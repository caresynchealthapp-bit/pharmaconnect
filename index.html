ABOUT PHARMACONNECT

Next-Generation Inventory & POS Infrastructure for Modern Pharmacies

PharmaConnect is an enterprise multi-tenant pharmacy management ecosystem designed to streamline pharmaceutical inventory, batch expiry tracking, supplier Goods Received Notes (GRN), and rapid cashier billing under one unified cloud platform.

Role-Isolated Access Control

Dedicated portals for Pharmacy Admins, POS Cashiers, and GRN Inventory Officers.

Geolocation & Multi-Branch Ready

Built-in GPS coordinates and location indexing for future customer medicine availability discovery.

Automated GRN & Expiry Alerts

Track batch numbers, supplier invoices, wholesale discounts, and near-expiry pharmaceuticals effortlessly.
